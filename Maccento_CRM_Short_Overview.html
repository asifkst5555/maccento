Executive Short Overview

Maccento CRM

A modern business operating system for real estate media companies that brings lead capture, follow-up, quoting, invoicing, project tracking, delivery, and client communication into one polished workflow.

What It Does

Maccento CRM helps teams respond faster, stay organized, deliver work more professionally, and create a stronger client experience from first inquiry to final handoff.

Front-End Experience

  • Public website with premium service presentation
  • AI chat assistance for faster inquiry handling
  • Package builder and contact form intake
  • Professional brand experience from first touch

Back-End Operations

  • Lead management and sales pipeline control
  • Quotes, invoices, and payment tracking
  • Project workflow and media delivery coordination
  • Client communication and internal team visibility

Core Business Value

Area Business Impact
Lead Capture Converts website traffic into structured business opportunities instead of loose inquiries.
Sales Follow-Up Gives the team a cleaner way to track status, contact history, and next actions.
Project Control Keeps active work visible from planning through shooting, editing, and completion.
Billing Improves payment visibility and supports more disciplined invoice management.
Delivery Creates a more professional handoff process for media galleries, final ZIP files, and client access.

The platform is designed to feel premium on the outside and practical on the inside: a marketing-facing front end for clients and a structured operations engine for the team.

How the Workflow Feels

  • A prospect lands on the website and gets clear service guidance.
  • The inquiry enters the system in an organized way.
  • The team reviews, follows up, and moves the opportunity forward.
  • Once approved, the work becomes a tracked project with billing and delivery control.
  • The client receives a more polished, transparent, and professional experience.

For Clients
